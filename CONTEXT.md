# Light Raiders

A PvPvE extraction shooter with an angled top-down view: Raiders descend into a world lit by a salvation that worked too well, stealing light from among the Choir — and everything they carry is at risk until they extract.

## Lore

**The Light**:
The never-setting radiance that ignited when the Hymn was sung. The most valuable substance in the world and its greatest danger: preserving, transformative, and hypnotic — it pulls at everyone who works near it. The religious resonance of all lore is structural, never stated.

**The Hymn**:
Humanity's last salvation broadcast — the song its final machine sang. It ignited the Light, and the Choir emerged from it. Why it was sung and what answered is the game's central mystery, revealed piecewise through Projects and never fully resolved ahead of the writing.

**Raider**:
A player character: one who leaves the shadowed places to steal light from the lit world and return alive.
_Avoid_: Scavenger, operator, mercenary

**The Fold**:
The home settlement in shadow. Houses the Stash, Workbench, Recycler, and Trader; all between-raid life happens here.
_Avoid_: Base, hub, hideout, home

**Lumen**:
The currency: light made spendable. Earned chiefly by selling Valuables to the Trader.
_Avoid_: Coins, credits, money

## Core Loop

**Raid**:
One play session on a map — insert, fight and loot, then extract or die. All progression happens between raids; all risk happens inside one.
_Avoid_: Match, run, session, game

**Stash**:
The player's persistent home inventory between raids. Never at risk; death cannot touch it.
_Avoid_: Bank, storage, inventory (ambiguous with in-raid inventory)

**Safe Pocket**:
A small set of inventory slots whose contents always survive the raid, even on death. Everything else carried is lost on death and lootable from the corpse.
_Avoid_: Secure container, safe box

**Escalation**:
The steady rise in the AI faction's numbers, aggression, and quality over a raid's lifetime. There is no raid clock; Escalation is what ends raids. Calling an Exfil spikes it.
_Avoid_: Difficulty ramp, heat, timer

**Exfil**:
The default extraction: called at a fixed site, followed by a loud, defended wait before departure.
_Avoid_: Evac, extract (verb is fine; not as the noun for this method)

**Hatch**:
The instant extraction: a locked exit scattered around the map that consumes a Hatch Key and extracts after a short interruptible channel, quietly.
_Avoid_: Escape hatch, trapdoor

**Hatch Key**:
A rare, one-use item — lootable and expensively craftable — that opens a Hatch.
_Avoid_: Access card, keycard

**Layer**:
A discrete space connected to other Layers only by doors; nothing can be seen or shot across a Layer boundary. The Surface is a Layer; every interior is a Layer. Players may always follow players through doors; AI enters Large interiors only.
_Avoid_: Zone, room, instance, floor

**Surface**:
The open-air outdoor Layer of a map — the warzone where most long-range combat and AI patrols live.
_Avoid_: Overworld, exterior

**Undercroft**:
The connective Layer beneath a map's Surface: a tunnel network with entrances in each district. Faster than surface travel, but dark, cramped, and contested.
_Avoid_: Tunnels, sewers, underground, catacombs

**Waygate**:
A fixed teleporter linking two points on a map. Only 1–3 exist per map, and any player's use puts that gate on a shared cooldown.
_Avoid_: Teleporter, portal

## The Choir

**The Choir**:
The hostile AI faction: powerful, long-lived psychic beings of light. Its unit ranks borrow angelic-hierarchy words that read as sci-fi (Powers, Virtues, Dominions, Thrones, Principalities). The buried truth — implied, never confirmed on screen: they were human once, the first to reach the Light, and they consumed and usurped more of it than anyone should. They guard it like addicts guard a supply, and they recognize what Raiders are becoming. The religious subtext is never made explicit anywhere in the game.
_Avoid_: Protoss, angels, aliens, machines

**Chorister**:
The Choir's smallest unit: a fast, fragile ground swarmer that attacks in melee packs.

**Power**:
The Choir's rank-and-file ground soldier: patrols the Surface and casts ranged psychic bolts.

**Herald**:
A fragile flying unit that avoids combat and sings to summon reinforcements, feeding Escalation. The priority target.

**Dominion**:
A slow, heavily armored ground unit with a devastating beam attack. Its armor plates have weak points.

**Throne**:
A rare floating elite that manifests only at high Escalation and actively hunts players. The reason raids end.

**Virtue / Principality / Seraph**:
Reserved post-slice ranks: Virtues (support/healers), Principalities (zone commanders), a Seraph (eventual raid-boss-tier event).

## Economy

**Workbench**:
The between-raid crafting station at the player's base. Crafting happens only here (in-raid crafting is deferred, not rejected).

**Recycler**:
The between-raid station that breaks any item into Materials. What makes junk worth carrying home.
_Avoid_: Scrapper, salvage

**Loot families**:
Every item belongs to one of six: Materials (craft inputs), Valuables (exist to be recycled or sold), Equipment (guns, shields, backpacks), Consumables (healing, utility), Choir Remnants, Relics.

**Choir Remnant**:
A part harvested from a Choir corpse — the only crafting source of Choir-tech weapons.
_Avoid_: Enemy parts, essence, cores

**Relic**:
A rare item dropped by elite Choir units with a rule-bending effect (never a flat stat boost). The game's "magic items."
_Avoid_: Artifact, trinket, charm

**Trader**:
The sell-only vendor at base: buys Valuables for currency, sells a small fixed catalog of basics (ammo, meds, tier-1 gear) as the floor against gear death-spirals.
_Avoid_: Shop, merchant, market

## Player kit

**Shield**:
The player's protective bar over health. Depletes before health and never regenerates on its own — it refills only from Shield Cells.
_Avoid_: Armor, barrier, overshield

**Shield Cell**:
The consumable that refills the Shield. The per-fight attrition cost that ties combat to the loot economy.
_Avoid_: Battery, charge

**Choir-tech Weapon**:
A gun craftable only from Choir Remnants, firing slower, brighter, harder-hitting bolts of the same character the Choir fires at players.
_Avoid_: Laser weapon, energy weapon, alien weapon

## Progression

**Skill Tree**:
Per-account progression whose nodes improve utility and economy only — movement, stamina, carry capacity, healing speed, craft/recycle yield, harvesting. Never +damage or +health; a new player can win any gunfight they aim better in.

**Project**:
A long-horizon construction goal at base, fed with specific materials across many raids, permanently unlocking infrastructure. Projects are also the lore-delivery spine: each completed Project advances the mystery of the Light and the Choir.
_Avoid_: Quest, mission

**Trial**:
A discrete in-raid challenge contract (objectives like "extract via a Hatch twice") rewarding skill XP, blueprints, or cosmetics. The session-to-session direction-giver.
_Avoid_: Challenge, contract, daily
